Rowe Livestock

My story starts when I was about twelve years old. I became fascinated with the life of a cowboy and a rancher. I grew up around the lifestyle, watching the work and the people who lived it, but I never had the chance to step into it myself — not until I turned sixteen. Once I did, I fell in love with every part of it. Feeding cows, working cattle, fencing, haying — all of it. It is a demanding way of life, but it is honest, rewarding, and deeply satisfying. Unlike many folks around me, I was not handed down a ranch. I am building mine from nothing. My start came four years ago when I bought my first bottle calf. She turned out to be one of the best mama cows I could have asked for, always raising strong, good looking calves. She was the beginning of my dream becoming real. The growth has been slow, but steady. Today, I am proud to offer high quality beef and pork in halves or wholes. And with the start of the farmers market in Alliance, NE, I will be bringing individual cuts of meat so customers can try my product before committing to a larger purchase. My operation is stress free, hormone free, and ethical. The animals are raised right, and the meat speaks for itself. It is some of the best you will ever eat..
